Today's brief Am I affected?

Version matching is approximate. A dotted version is compared with a shortened list of version ranges from NVD. Many exploited bugs in the CISA catalog have no version range, so a name match still needs a person to confirm it. This page does not send your inventory anywhere.

The sample is a 30-second demo: Exchange, Windows, WordPress, FortiOS, RouterOS, a TP-Link model, a Yoco card machine, and a printer that should not match. Columns are name, vendor, version.

A match means the name or version looks close to a public record. It does not mean the bug is present on your network. Missing software is invisible to this page. This is not an audit and not a certification.
